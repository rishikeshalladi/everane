Install Everane as an app
Install Everane on your device to enable push notifications and get an app icon on your home screen.
To install on iPhone / iPad:
  1. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom of Safari
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add in the top-right
  4. Open Everane from your Home Screen icon
  5. Come back to this page and enable Push reminders

📱 SMS Reminders — Your Consent

Before you add your phone number, please review what you'll receive and agree to receive SMS text messages.

What you'll receive:
  • Medication dose reminders at the times you schedule
  • Missed-dose alerts if you don't mark a dose taken
  • Your daily medication agenda each morning
  • One-time verification codes when you add or change your phone number
What we will NOT do:
  • No marketing or promotional messages — ever
  • No third-party sharing of your phone number
  • No selling your information
Message frequency depends on your medication schedule. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help. You can also remove your phone number from this profile at any time.

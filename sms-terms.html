SMS Terms & Opt-In Consent

This page describes the SMS (text message) program operated by Everane, including what messages we send, how users opt in, how to opt out, and our commitments around privacy and data handling. It is provided as a public reference so that anyone — including users, caregivers, and messaging carrier reviewers — can understand exactly how our SMS program works without needing an account.

Program type Everane sends transactional SMS messages only — medication reminders, missed-dose alerts, daily medication agendas, and one-time verification codes. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages. We do not share or sell phone numbers.

1. Program description

Everane is a medication reminder application. After a user creates an account at https://everane.live/, they can choose to add a phone number to their profile and opt in to receive SMS reminders for medications they have scheduled in the app. The same SMS program is also used by caregivers (people managing medication on behalf of another patient) for adherence alerts and summaries.

2. Messages users will receive after opting in

Patients who opt in may receive the following message types:

  • Medication dose reminders at the times the user has scheduled in their app.
  • Missed-dose alerts if a scheduled dose is not marked as taken within roughly 45 minutes of the scheduled time.
  • Daily medication agenda — a short summary of the day's scheduled doses, delivered each morning.
  • One-time verification codes when the user adds or changes their phone number.

Caregivers who opt in may also receive:

  • Alerts when a patient they care for misses doses.
  • Adherence summaries (for example, an alert if a patient's adherence drops below 80%).
  • Weekly and monthly report notifications.
  • Bottle expiration or refill alerts for their patients.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency depends entirely on the user's own medication schedule. A user who schedules one daily dose with a single advance reminder will receive roughly two messages per day; a user with multiple medications across the day will receive more. Typical message volume is between 1 and 10 SMS messages per day per user. Verification codes are sent only when a phone number is added or changed.

4. Message and data rates

Standard message and data rates may apply, depending on the user's mobile carrier and plan. Everane does not charge a separate fee for SMS messages.

5. Opt-in flow (how users consent)

Consent is obtained directly inside the Everane web app at the moment the user provides a phone number. It is a deliberate, two-step process:

  1. The user signs in to their profile at https://everane.live/profile.html (patients) or https://everane.live/caregiver/profile.html (caregivers) and clicks “Add a phone number.”
  2. Before any phone number can be entered, a SMS Consent modal opens. The modal lists every message type the user will receive, states that Everane sends no marketing and shares no phone numbers, describes message frequency and that message and data rates may apply, and includes opt-out (STOP) and help (HELP) instructions.
  3. The user must check a required consent checkbox that reads, verbatim:
Exact opt-in language (checkbox label on the consent modal)
“I agree to receive recurring SMS text messages from Everane at the phone number I provide for medication reminders, missed-dose alerts, agendas, and verification codes. I have read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. I understand I can reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time.”

Only after the checkbox is ticked does the “I Agree — Continue” button become enabled. Tapping it records the consent (timestamp, consent version, user agent, and method) on the user's own Firestore profile document under the field smsConsent, and only then is the user able to enter their phone number and verify it via a one-time SMS code.

After consent is recorded, the user's profile page persistently displays a confirmation note such as “✓ You agreed to receive SMS reminders on March 14, 2026. Reply STOP to any text to unsubscribe.” as ongoing proof of opt-in.

6. Opt-out (STOP)

Users can stop SMS messages at any time by replying STOP (or any standard opt-out keyword recognised by the messaging carrier, such as STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT) to any SMS sent by Everane. Once the carrier processes the opt-out, our system will be blocked from sending further SMS to that number. Users can also remove their phone number from their profile in the Everane web app at any time to stop messages.

7. Help (HELP)

Users can reply HELP at any time to receive a brief response with contact information. For account-level assistance, users can also email support@everane.live.

9. What we do NOT do

  • No marketing or promotional SMS. Everane never sends sales, promotional, fundraising, or advertising messages over SMS.
  • No third-party sharing. We do not share, lease, rent, or sell phone numbers to any third party.
  • No cross-selling. We do not use SMS to promote unrelated products or services.
  • No SMS to users who have not opted in. SMS is only sent to users who have completed the in-app opt-in flow described above.

10. Eligibility and supported regions

The Everane SMS program is currently available to users in the United States. Users must be 18 or older (or the parent/guardian of a minor) to enable SMS reminders. By opting in, the user confirms they own or are authorised to use the phone number they provide.

11. Privacy and data handling

Phone numbers provided for SMS reminders are stored on the user's own profile document in Everane's database and are used only to send the SMS message types described in this document. Phone numbers are not shared with third parties for any purpose other than the actual delivery of SMS messages via our messaging carrier (Twilio Inc.). For full details about how Everane handles personal information, please see our Privacy Policy.

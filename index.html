Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you do not.

A focused set of tools for keeping medications on schedule, day after day.

F.01📋

Medication dashboard

Every medication, dose, and schedule in one place. Mark doses taken with a tap.

F.02⏰

Reminders

Email reminders and instant push notifications at the times you set, before each dose, at the dose, or after a missed one.

F.03📦

Refill tracking

Log a new bottle once and Everane counts down as you take doses, with warnings before the bottle runs out.

F.04📷

Snap a photo

Photograph a note, an email, or the bottle itself. Everane reads the details and fills in the form for you.

F.05🎤

Voice add

Tap the mic and describe a medication out loud. The form fills itself in. Review and save.

F.06👨‍👩‍👧

Caregivers

Invite family members. They can see your adherence and receive forwarded reminders, read only, no editing.

F.07🔒

Private by default

Data is stored in Google Firebase, encrypted in transit and at rest. Not shared, not sold.

F.08🌍

Travels with you

Schedules respect your time zone and adjust when you travel, so reminders land at the right local time.

F.09📈

Adherence stats

See your streaks, missed doses, and trends across the week in a calendar and chart view.
